About

Growth marketing and analytics across four years and five markets.

I started in marketing in Mumbai, running creator and paid social campaigns for brands like AB InBev and BookMyShow. Two years ago I left India and moved to the US for my Master's in Global Management. Since then I've produced social content for Arizona State University, grown marketing pages from zero at Thunderbird, and built go-to-market campaigns for Hexafort, always chasing the same question: what's actually working, and why?

I'm targeting marketing roles in growth and performance marketing, demand generation, and marketing analytics, the throughline across every role above. Outside of work: dancing, filming travel videos, and a few too many hours of The West Wing — that move taught me I have more grit than I give myself credit for, mostly borrowed from my parents' optimism.
